\begin{frame}{Current Status: Lesson Program Adoption}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Adoption as an official lesson program of The Carpentries was planned for 1 September 2026
		\item Changes in Carpentries Core Team support called for a short delay
		\item Adoption will be completed between now and November 2026
	\end{itemize}
\note{Those of you who attended my talk last year might recall that the big news back then was that HPC Carpentry would be adopted by The Carpentries, becoming their fourth official lesson program. The launch date for the new program was set for 1st September this year, which was last week. Unfortunately, that timeline had to be adjusted earlier this summer, after The Carpentries announced a reduction in Core Team capacity. We were all really keen to make it happen, though, and most of the work for the adoption had already been done, so the delay should be quite short. The deadline is now 1 November this year, and we are optimistic that we will be able to complete the process before then. Watch out for further announcements on The Carpentries blog.}
\end{frame}
